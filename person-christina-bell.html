Christina Bell

Ph.D. Physics Candidate

About

Christina graduated from Arizona State University in 2024, where she measured and characterized the non-linear kinetic inductance of high-Tc thin-film superconducting devices. She also spent time working on the low-level RF system for ASU’s CXFEL. In the Irwin Group, she is primarily involved with the design, fabrication, and characterization of Radio-frequency Quantum Upconverters.
