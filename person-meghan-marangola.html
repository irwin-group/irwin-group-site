Meghan Marangola

Ph.D. Physics Student

About

Meghan graduated from Harvard University in 2025. As an undergraduate, she constructed and installed an instrument to measure image quality degradation at the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, and developed components of the control system for the ATLAS ITk strip detector upgrade for the HL-LHC. She is now part of the team commissioning the DMRadio-50L experiment, and she works on integrating components and improving noise performance in the SQUID detection and readout chain.
