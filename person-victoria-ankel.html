Victoria (Tori) Ankel

Ph.D. Physics Candidate

About

Tori graduated from UChicago in 2023, where she primarily worked on the Payload for Ultrahigh Energy Observations (PUEO) experiment—a search for ultra-high energy astrophysical neutrinos using their interaction with Antarctic ice. At Stanford, her work focuses on the design and construction of DMRadio-50L and DMRadio-m3.
