Cady van Assendelft

Ph.D. Physics Candidate
Quantum Sensors

About

Cady graduated from Yale University in 2019 with a B.S. in Physics. As an undergraduate, she worked with David Moore on a levitated microsphere experiment testing gravity at microscopic scales, and then joined the HAYSTAC axion dark matter experiment, where she prototyped components for its second phase. In the Irwin Group, she develops quantum sensors for DMRadio, including studies of dc SQUID damping in superconducting resonant circuits and the modeling and characterization of Radio-frequency Quantum Upconverters. She defended her Ph.D. thesis in 2026.
