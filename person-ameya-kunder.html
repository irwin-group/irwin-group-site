Ameya Kunder

Ph.D. Physics Candidate

About

Ameya graduated from UC Berkeley in 2023 with a B.A. in Physics and Computer Science. As an undergraduate, he worked with Benjamin Safdi on simulations of axions in the early universe. In the Irwin Group, he works on Radio-frequency Quantum Upconverters, including circuit simulation and the study of noise and frequency upconversion in the devices.
