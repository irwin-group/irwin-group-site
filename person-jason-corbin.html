Jason Corbin

Ph.D. Physics Candidate

About

Jason received a B.S. in Physics from UC Santa Barbara in 2020, where he worked on the design and fabrication of Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detectors. He has since transitioned towards quantum sensing for axion detection. He is currently fabricating and testing radio-frequency quantum upconverters, with some involvement in the design of DMRadio-50L.

Jason is supported by the HEPCAT Fellowship.
