About

Alex Droster is a KIPAC Postdoctoral Fellow at Stanford and SLAC working on the DMRadio axion dark matter experiment and with the DMQIS group on SQUATs, a new architecture for qubit-based detectors. He earned a Ph.D. in Nuclear Engineering from UC Berkeley in 2025, where he contributed to the HAYSTAC, DMRadio, and ALPHA dark matter searches, and in 2016 earned A.B.’s in Physics and Mathematics from the University of Chicago. Before graduate school, he worked at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory on the POLARBEAR-2 cosmic microwave background experiment, where he helped design a cryogenic rotating half-wave plate now operating in Chile’s Atacama Desert. His research interests include experimental astrophysics, instrumentation, quantum sensing, and nuclear nonproliferation.
